# Integrations

The **Integrations** tab connects Shoko to other services that scrobble your media. Trakt support was removed in Shoko
Server v6.

## Plex Options (Legacy)

Click **Authenticate**, then **Login**, and sign in to Plex to link your account. Then choose your Plex **Server** and tick the
**Available Libraries** Shoko should track. Server and library selections are saved right away. **Unlink** disconnects your Plex
account.

Linking lets Shoko mark episodes as watched when you finish them in Plex. Users who are **not Plex Pass**
members need to sync their watch states with the **Sync Plex Watch Status** action or a
[Utility Script (Legacy)](/plex/legacy-agents-and-scanners/syncing-watched-states#using-a-utility-script-advanced). See
[Syncing Watched States (Legacy)](/plex/legacy-agents-and-scanners/syncing-watched-states).
